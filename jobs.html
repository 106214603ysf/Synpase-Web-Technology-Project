Our Commitment to Inclusion

Synapse acknowledges the Traditional Custodians of the lands where we work and pays respect to Elders past and present.

We encourage applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples. Health services work best when the people who build them reflect the communities they serve, so we offer culturally safe workplaces and flexible leave for cultural and community obligations.

Why Work With Us?

Synapse builds online patient platforms, appointment booking systems and health information websites. Our team works in a hybrid setup from our Melbourne office, with mentoring, paid training and flexible hours.

Front-End Web Developer

Develop and maintain responsive, accessible pages for patient booking and health information platforms using HTML5 and CSS3.

Reference number
FE001
Salary
$85,000 – $100,000 per year plus superannuation
Reports to
Head of Digital Engineering
Location
Melbourne VIC (hybrid)
Employment type
Full-time

Key Responsibilities

  1. Build responsive web pages from approved designs.
  2. Test pages using keyboard navigation and screen readers.
  3. Validate HTML and CSS and fix accessibility issues.
  4. Follow secure coding practices to protect patient privacy.

Essential Requirements

  • Strong knowledge of HTML5 and CSS3.
  • Understanding of web accessibility principles (WCAG 2.1).
  • Experience using Git and GitHub.

Preferable Requirements

  • Experience working in healthcare technology.
  • Familiarity with Jira and Agile sprints.

UX Designer – Patient Experience

Design clear and inclusive experiences for patients booking appointments and accessing health information online.

Reference number
UX002
Salary
$90,000 – $105,000 per year plus superannuation
Reports to
Design Lead, Patient Platforms
Location
Melbourne VIC (hybrid)
Employment type
Full-time

Key Responsibilities

  1. Research patient needs through interviews and usability testing.
  2. Create wireframes and prototypes for new features.
  3. Write clear labels and helpful error messages.
  4. Check designs for colour contrast and readability.

Essential Requirements

  • Experience with prototyping tools such as Figma.
  • Understanding of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG).
  • Experience with user research and usability testing.

Preferable Requirements

  • Background in healthcare or digital health platforms.
  • Experience working with front-end developers in Agile teams.
