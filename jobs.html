Our Commitment to Inclusion

Synapse acknowledges the Traditional Custodians of the lands where we work and pays respect to Elders past and present.

We strongly encourage applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples, because culturally safe health services start with a team that reflects the communities we serve.

Why Work With Us

Synapse builds online patient platforms, appointment systems and health information websites. We are growing our web team and want people who care about clear, accessible and trustworthy healthcare technology.

Two positions are open. Applications close on 30 November 2026.

Front-End Web Developer

Build and maintain the patient-facing pages of our booking and health information platforms using HTML5 and CSS3.

Reference number
FE001
Salary
$85,000 - $100,000 per year plus superannuation
Reports to
Head of Digital Engineering

Key Responsibilities

  1. Develop responsive web pages from approved designs.
  2. Test pages with screen readers and keyboard navigation.
  3. Validate all code and fix accessibility issues.
  4. Follow secure practices to protect patient privacy.

Essential Requirements

  • Strong knowledge of HTML5 and CSS3.
  • Understanding of web accessibility principles.
  • Experience using Git and GitHub.

Preferable Requirements

  • Experience working in healthcare.
  • Familiarity with Jira and Agile sprints.

UX Designer - Patient Experience

Design simple, inclusive journeys for booking appointments and accessing health information online.

Reference number
UX002
Salary
$90,000 - $105,000 per year plus superannuation
Reports to
Design Lead, Patient Platforms

Key Responsibilities

  1. Research patient needs through interviews and usability testing.
  2. Create wireframes and prototypes for new features.
  3. Write plain-language labels and error messages.
  4. Check finished pages for colour contrast and readability.

Essential Requirements

  • Proficiency in wireframing and prototyping tools (e.g., Figma).
  • Solid understanding of accessibility standards (WCAG).
  • Experience conducting user research and usability testing.

Preferable Requirements

  • Background in healthcare or digital health platforms.
  • Experience working alongside front-end developers in Agile teams.
